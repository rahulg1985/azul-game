Azul

Tile Game

How would you like to play?

Goal: score the most points by tiling your wall.

Each turn: take all tiles of one color from a factory or the center (leftovers in a factory move to the center; the first player to take from the center also gets the 1st-player marker and a −1 penalty), then place them on one pattern line — or drop them on your floor line if they don’t fit.

End of round: each completed pattern line moves one tile onto your wall, scoring 1 point plus 1 for each tile it touches horizontally and vertically. Floor tiles cost penalties (−1, −1, −2, −2, −2, −3, −3).

Game end: the game finishes after a round in which someone completes a horizontal wall row. Bonuses: +2 per full row, +7 per full column, +10 per color with all 5 placed.
