Tile Game

How would you like to play?

🎯 The goal

Decorate your palace wall with tiles and score the most points. The game ends after the round in which any player completes a full horizontal row of 5 tiles on their wall.

🧩 Your board

  • Pattern lines (left) — 5 rows that hold 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 tiles. This is where tiles wait before going on the wall.
  • Wall (right) — a 5 × 5 grid. Each space only accepts the tile color printed on it.
  • Floor line (bottom) — where tiles you can't place end up. Every tile there costs you points.

1️⃣ Factory offer — take turns drafting

On your turn, take tiles in one of these ways:

  • From a factory: tap any one color in a factory disc to take all tiles of that color. The tiles left behind slide into the center.
  • From the center: tap a color in the center pile to take all tiles of that color there. The first player to take from the center each round also gets the “1” starting-player marker — it sits on your floor line (a −1 penalty) and you will start the next round.

Then place the tiles you took onto one pattern line:

  • A line holds one color only and fills up from the right.
  • You can't pick a line if that color is already on the matching row of your wall.
  • Tiles that don't fit drop onto your floor line — or tap the floor line to put them there directly.

Players keep taking turns until every factory and the center are empty.

2️⃣ Wall-tiling — score your completed lines

  • Going top to bottom, each complete pattern line moves its rightmost tile onto the space of the same color in that row of your wall, and scores right away. The rest of that line's tiles are discarded.
  • Scoring a tile: if it touches no other tile, it scores 1 point. Otherwise count all tiles linked to it horizontally, add all tiles linked vertically (count the new tile in both), and score that total.
  • Floor line: each tile there costs the points printed above its space: −1, −1, −2, −2, −2, −3, −3. The starting marker counts as a floor tile. Your score never goes below 0.
  • When the score card appears, tap it to start the next round. The player with the starting marker goes first, and the factories refill — 5 factories for 2 players, 7 for 3, 9 for 4.

🏁 End of the game — bonuses

  • +2 points for each complete horizontal row of 5 on your wall
  • +7 points for each complete vertical column of 5
  • +10 points for each color of which you placed all 5 tiles

Most points wins. If tied, the player with more complete horizontal rows wins — if still tied, the win is shared.

📱 Playing in this app

  • Tap a tile group to pick it up, then tap a glowing pattern line (or your floor line) to place it. Tap the group again to put it back.
  • Local: pass-and-play on this device. Online: create a room and share the invite link — voice chat is on by default.
  • The 🔊 button in the game turns the sounds and voice announcements on or off.
