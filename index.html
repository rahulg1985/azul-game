The Tile-Laying Game

How would you like to play?

Azul is a game of beautiful tiles and careful choices. Draft tiles from the factories, complete pattern lines, and build the most beautiful wall in the palace.

🎯 Objective

Score the most points by tiling your wall. The game ends after the round in which a player completes a full horizontal row of five tiles — final bonuses are then added, and the highest score wins.

🧩 The Board

  • Pattern lines — Five rows holding 1 to 5 tiles, where drafted tiles wait their turn.
  • Wall — A 5 × 5 grid; every space accepts only the color printed on it.
  • Floor line — Where surplus tiles land. Every tile here costs points.

1️⃣ Draft — The Factory Offer

Players take turns. On your turn, choose one:

  • Factory: Take all tiles of a single color from one factory. The remaining tiles slide into the center.
  • Center: Take all tiles of a single color from the center. The first player to draw from the center each round also takes the starting-player marker — it costs 1 point on the floor line, but you lead the next round.

Then place your tiles on a single pattern line:

  • One color per line, and lines fill from the right.
  • A color already on that row of your wall cannot be drafted into the line.
  • Overflow drops to your floor line — or send tiles straight there.

The phase ends when every factory and the center stand empty.

2️⃣ Tile the Wall — Scoring

When drafting ends, completed lines tile the wall, top line first:

  • The rightmost tile of each full line moves to its color’s space on the wall and scores immediately; the rest of the line is discarded.
  • Scoring: A tile with no neighbors scores 1 point. Otherwise, count its full horizontal row of linked tiles and its full vertical column — the new tile counts in both — and add them together.
  • Floor line: Tiles cost −1, −1, −2, −2, −2, −3, −3 from left to right, and the starting marker counts as one tile. Scores never fall below zero.

Tap the round card to continue. The factories refill — five for two players, seven for three, nine for four.

🏁 Final Scoring

  • +2 — Each completed horizontal row of five
  • +7 — Each completed vertical column of five
  • +10 — Each color placed in all five of its spaces

Ties break on completed horizontal rows; a perfect tie is a shared victory.

📱 In This App

  • Controls: Tap a tile group to lift it, then tap a glowing pattern line — or the floor line — to set it down. Tap the group again to change your mind.
  • Modes: Local is pass-and-play on one device. Online rooms seat friends anywhere through a shareable link, with voice chat built in.
  • Sound: The speaker button toggles tile sounds and spoken announcements.
